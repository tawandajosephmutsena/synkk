## Launch Tiers
- **Starter:** Free self-hosted tier for solo builders and small experiments.
- **Team Pro:** Flat workspace pricing — zero per-seat penalty as teams grow.
- **Enterprise:** Dedicated deployment assistance, custom SLAs, and compliance support.
